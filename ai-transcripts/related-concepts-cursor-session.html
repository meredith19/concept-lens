The seed is three isolated clusters. The only honest SAME_MEANING bridge is a physical logistics event that return/refund eligibility can actually include. Country cannot join without stuffing country-shaped meaning onto a concept that is not about country, or adding a fact on BuyerCountry/BillingCountry and breaking complete equivalence.

Adding a fact on Shipped, BuyerCountry, or BillingCountry would change one of the three derived relationships. Adding on Returnable, Refundable, or Delivered would not.

There is no valid “new fact on Delivered mapped to an existing Returnable/Refundable fact”: none of those existing facts mean “left the FC” or “arrived.” So the new fact has to live on Returns or Payments and map onto an existing delivery/ship fact.

Option 1 (recommended) — Returnable includes receipt

New fact on returns.returnable:

{
  "id": "returns.returnable.item_received",
  "label": "Item has been received",
  "description": "The item has arrived at the destination and is in the customer's possession."

Maps to: delivery.delivered.reached_destination (“Reached the destination” — The package arrived at the destination address.)

Why SAME_MEANING is valid: Both statements are the same event: the package arrived at the destination. Returns cares because a return is initiated against an item the customer actually has; Delivery cares because that event ends the delivery leg. Same pattern as rel_024 (Fulfillment vs Delivery observing dispatch).

Why it belongs on Returnable: Returnable is whether an item can currently be returned. Path, window, and not-final-sale are incomplete if the item never arrived (that is usually a cancel, not a return). Receipt is a real eligibility conjunct, not a graph hack.

What not to do
  • New fact on Shipped mapped outward: Shipped would no longer be fully matched vs Delivered → containment becomes NOT_ESTABLISHED.
  • New fact on BuyerCountry or BillingCountry: complete equivalence becomes containment.
  • Map anything to country facts: payment-instrument country, ISO normalization, and null-defaulting are not what Returnable, Refundable, Shipped, or Delivered mean.
  • Map within_return_window to reached_destination: window vs event, not SAME_MEANING.
  • Map return_approved across clusters: that stays inside Returns/Payments, and approval ≠ delivery.
